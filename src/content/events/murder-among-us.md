## About the Event

Murder Among Us is an immersive, fast-paced murder mystery game that turns an ordinary classroom into a crime scene filled with secrets, deception, clues, and suspicion.

Participants step into roles of different characters connected to a mysterious murder. Each player receives a confidential character script containing their identity, background, secrets, and relationships.

**The twist:** One participant is secretly the murderer.

The murderer must blend in, manipulate conversations, and eliminate players without being caught. The remaining participants must investigate, discover hidden clues, connect evidence, and expose the killer.

---

## Gameplay

### 1. Enter the Crime Scene

~20 participants enter a classroom transformed into a crime scene with hidden clues, suspicious objects, and evidence connected to the murder.

### 2. Receive Your Character

Each participant receives a sealed envelope with their character script containing:

- Character name and identity
- Relationship with the victim
- Secrets that must be protected
- Information about other characters
- Possible motives and clues

### 3. The Hidden Murderer

One randomly selected participant receives a special Murderer's Script and a murder sticker. The murderer must secretly eliminate other players by placing the sticker on them without being noticed.

### 4. Investigate the Crime

Players can question others, exchange information, search for hidden clues, compare stories, discuss motives, form alliances, and accuse suspicious characters.

### 5. The Murder

At any suitable moment, the murderer can secretly eliminate another participant. The more players disappear, the more pressure falls on the murderer.

### 6. The Final Investigation

Participants gather to discuss findings — evidence, statements, motives, contradictions, and timelines. The group must decide: **Who is the murderer?**

## Round Structure

| Stage | Time |
|-------|------|
| Character Assignment & Briefing | 1–2 min |
| Investigation | 3–5 min |
| Murder & Clue Discovery | Throughout |
| Final Discussion | 1–2 min |
| Accusation & Reveal | 1 min |
| **Total** | **5–10 min** |

## Participants

Recommended: **20 participants per round**. The game can be repeated across multiple rounds with different mysteries, characters, and storylines.
