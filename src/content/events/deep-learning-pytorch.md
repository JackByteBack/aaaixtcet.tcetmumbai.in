## Workshop Recap

A hands-on deep learning workshop where participants built and trained neural networks using PyTorch.

### What We Covered

- Introduction to PyTorch and tensor operations
- Building neural networks with `torch.nn`
- Training loops and optimization
- GPU acceleration for faster training
- Building a image classifier from scratch
