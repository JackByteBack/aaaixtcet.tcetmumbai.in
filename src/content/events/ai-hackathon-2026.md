## About the Hackathon

Put your AI skills to the test in this intensive 24-hour hackathon! Teams of 2-4 members will tackle real-world problems using artificial intelligence and machine learning.

### Theme

**AI for Good** — Build solutions that use AI to solve problems in education, healthcare, sustainability, or social impact.

### Schedule

- **Day 1, 10:00 AM** — Kickoff and problem statement reveal
- **Day 1, 11:00 AM** — Hacking begins
- **Day 2, 10:00 AM** — Hacking ends
- **Day 2, 11:00 AM** — Presentations and judging
- **Day 2, 1:00 PM** — Results and prize distribution

### Prizes

- **1st Place**: ₹25,000 + Trophies + Internship opportunities
- **2nd Place**: ₹15,000 + Trophies
- **3rd Place**: ₹10,000 + Trophies
- **Best Design**: Special prize

### Rules

- Teams of 2-4 members
- All team members must be TCET students
- Use of open-source libraries and APIs is allowed
- Projects must be built during the hackathon
- Existing projects or pre-built solutions are not allowed
